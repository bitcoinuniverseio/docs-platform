title: Choose your path
description: Entry points by audience, whether you browse, create, trade, build, operate, or verify.
---

Pick the row that describes you. Each path starts at the page written for that job.

| You want to | Start at |
| --- | --- |
| **Browse and understand** what exists on-chain | [Core documentation](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-core) and the [Protocol Atlas](/protocols/) |
| **Create** inscriptions, tokens, or mints | [Inscribe documentation](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-inscribe) |
| **Collect or trade** assets | [Core documentation](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-core) for the marketplace, [StampDEX documentation](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-stampdex) for Stamps |
| **Build an application** against our APIs | [Developer overview](/developers/) |
| **Implement a protocol** from its specification | [Protocol Atlas](/protocols/); each dossier links its normative specification and test vectors |
| **Operate an indexer** | The indexer repository for your protocol, catalogued in the [developer overview](/developers/) |
| **Verify a claim** made by us or anyone else | [Source provenance](/status/provenance/) and [How to read our status](/status/) |

Whatever the path, read [Safety in sixty seconds](/start/safety/) first. It is short on purpose.
