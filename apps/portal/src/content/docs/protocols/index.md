One row per protocol in the public estate. Each repository is the home of that protocol's specification; dossier pages on this portal are built from those repositories at pinned commits.

Lifecycle wording follows the [shared terminology](/status/). A protocol listed here is documented; whether a Universe product currently supports it live is a separate, evidence-backed claim made in that product's capability matrix, never inferred from this table.
