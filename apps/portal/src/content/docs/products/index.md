Every product Bitcoin Universe ships, with its documentation home. Lifecycle and availability wording follows the [shared status terminology](/status/); a product's own documentation is the authority on what it supports today.

## Applications

| Product | Purpose | Chains | Documentation | Live |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | Explorer, portfolio, activity, and marketplace across supported protocols | Bitcoin | [docs-core](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-core) | [bitcoinuniverse.io](https://bitcoinuniverse.io) |
| **Inscribe** | Creation studio: files, text, tokens, mints, protocol workflows | Bitcoin | [docs-inscribe](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-inscribe) | [inscribe.bitcoinuniverse.io](https://inscribe.bitcoinuniverse.io) |
| **Wallet** | Browser wallet for Bitcoin digital artifacts | Bitcoin | [docs-wallet](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-wallet) | See its documentation for release availability |
| **StampDEX** | Trading venue for Bitcoin Stamps assets | Bitcoin | [docs-stampdex](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-stampdex) | See its documentation |
| **Zerdinals and Z-Runes** | Digital-artifact record on Zcash: inscriptions, ZRunes, collections, explorer | Zcash | [docs-zerdinals-and-zrunes](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-zerdinals-and-zrunes) | [zrunes.io](https://zrunes.io) |
| **Forked Felines** | Collection with on-chain artwork, minting, and holder features | Bitcoin | [forked-felines-docs](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/forked-felines-docs) | [forked-felines.art](https://forked-felines.art) |
